# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** September 1, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the "JustClock" application for mobile devices.

## 1. Information Collection and Use

**We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.** 

The Fullscreen Clock application is designed to function entirely offline. It does not collect any personally identifiable information (PII), analytics, crash reports, or usage data. Your data stays completely on your device.

## 2. Local Storage

The application utilizes local storage on your device (such as Shared Preferences) to save your custom app settings. This includes your preferences for:
- Fullscreen mode toggle
- Custom color selections
- Timer and stopwatch states

This data is stored locally on your device and is never transmitted to our servers or any third parties. If you uninstall the application or clear its data, these settings will be permanently removed.

## 3. Device Permissions

To provide its core functionality, the application requests the following device permissions:
- **Wake Lock (Keep Screen On):** This permission is used strictly to prevent your device's screen from turning off while the clock, stopwatch, or timer is actively running on the screen.
- **Audio / Alarms:** Used to play ringtones or alarms when a timer reaches zero.

These permissions are used solely for the local operation of the app and do not involve data collection.

## 4. Third-Party Services

The application does not use any third-party services, analytics, tracking, or advertising frameworks that may collect your information.

## 5. Children's Privacy

These Services do not address anyone under the age of 13. Since we do not collect any personal information, we do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

## 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. These changes are effective immediately after they are posted.
